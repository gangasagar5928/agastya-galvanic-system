| Handling CuSO₄, NaOH | Above + nitrile gloves |
| Handling dilute acid (Cu prep) | Above + face shield if > 1 L; work in fume hood |

## 3. Ventilation

- **All CuSO₄ / NaOH / acid work:** in a ventilated fume cupboard or open lab with good airflow.
- **Electrolyzer operating:** outdoor or in well-ventilated room; H₂ rises and disperses quickly, but do not work in an enclosed box.

## 4. Procedure-Specific Safety Notes

### 4.1 De-resination of Sawdust
- Boil hardwood sawdust in distilled water for 2 h on a hotplate. Use a large beaker (at least 2× volume) to prevent boil-over.
- **Steam risk:** stand to the side when lifting lid; do not use hands to direct steam.

### 4.2 Copper Sulfate Solution (1.0 M CuSO₄)
- Dissolve CuSO₄·5H₂O in distilled water. Exothermic — add to water, not the reverse.
- Wear gloves; do not taste or inhale powder.
- **Spill procedure:** wet the powder with damp paper towel → scoop into labelled waste container → rinse area 3× with water. Do NOT dry-sweep (dust risk).

### 4.3 Sodium Hydroxide (for electrolyzer electrolyte)
- Dissolve pellets in water slowly (exothermic). Use a minimum 10× volume of water.
- Label the solution clearly as **NaOH dilute (0.1 M) — Corrosive**.
- Have dilute vinegar (acetic acid) available as a neutralizer on skin spill.

### 4.4 Electrolyzer – Hydrogen Gas
- H₂ is **flammable/explosive at 4–75 % concentration in air**.
- **Never enclose the electrolyzer in a sealed container** while running.
- Do not operate near open flames or sparks.
- In lab: run the electrolyzer in a fume hood or open bench with free airflow.
- After each run, vent any accumulated gas to atmosphere before disassembling.

### 4.5 Copper Plate Preparation (acid etch)
- If using dilute H₂SO₄ to clean Cu plates: work in fume hood, wear face shield.
- Rinse plates thoroughly with DM water after etch; neutralize used acid with dilute NaHCO₃ before drain disposal.

### 4.6 Spent Electrolyte Disposal
- **Do NOT pour CuSO₄ solution down the drain** — copper is an aquatic pollutant.
- Label collected spent solution as: **WASTE: CuSO₄(aq) — toxic to aquatic life**.
- Dispose via the institution's hazardous waste point, or neutralize with iron filings (cementation) before disposal. Ask lab coordinator.

## 5. Emergency Procedures

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| **Skin contact (CuSO₄)** | Rinse immediately under running water 15 min; wash with soap; seek medical advice if irritation persists |
| **Eye contact (any chemical)** | Irrigate with eyewash 15 min minimum; seek medical attention immediately |
| **Skin contact (NaOH)** | Flush 15 min with water; apply dilute vinegar (weak acid); **seek medical attention** |
| **Chemical spill on bench** | Contain with absorbent pad; neutralize acid/alkali; clean, wash, report |
| **H₂ leak / unusual smell** | Evacuate area; do not spark; ventilate; do not re-enter until clear |
| **Hot water burn** | Cool under cold running water 10–20 min; do not apply creams; seek medical for large burns |

> **Lab phone / emergency contact:** confirm with department before Phase 1 start.
